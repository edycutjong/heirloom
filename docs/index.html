Set It and Forget It · Reactive Omni Buildathon

Your wallet goes quiet. The vault remembers your partner.

If you die with crypto in your own wallet, nobody gets it. Heirloom's Reactive contract watches your everyday transfers; when they stop for the period you set, a grace window opens on Base, any transfer or vault action of yours cancels it, and then your heir claims. No check-in button. No keeper.

How it works

Silence starts the clock.

There is no server. A reactive contract on Lasna subscribes to your token transfers on each watched chain, keeps time with Reactive's own Cron, and calls back into a small vault on Base.

  1. Your token Transfer on Base, Ethereum or Unichain
  2. react() on Lasna: filter, then stamp lastSeen
  3. Cron: silence ran out → callback
  4. HeirloomVault on Base: grace → claimable
  5. Heir: initiateClaim → finalizeClaim

Silence is the signal

Every value-carrying transfer of an allowlisted token out of your address, on each watched chain, is a visit stamp. You keep using your wallet; there is nothing to press. The same transfer at $0 is struck through.

Questions a judge would ask.

What counts as being alive?

A value-carrying transfer of a token on your allowlist, out of your address, on a chain you watch; or an action you take on the vault itself. A $0 transfer never counts.

What if I just go travelling for a month?

You choose the silence period at deploy; the long-running testnet instance uses 90 days. Silence first opens a grace window, and any transfer or vault action during it cancels. You can withdraw from the vault at any time before release.

Can a stranger fake my life, or cause my release?

Neither. $0 transfers are refused and logged, and tokens you have not listed are never subscribed, so neither can keep you alive; and only your silence can start a release. The worst a spammer can do is hold a grace open (Remark 1).

What if the Reactive Network stops hearing a chain?

An owner-independent Chainlink ETH/USD feed acts as a canary. If its updates stop arriving, the countdown freezes once, capped, so a release never fires on a silence nobody measured. The Ethereum feeds measured so far update about hourly (medians 3,612 s and 3,636 s over about 23 h).

What is real, and what is not yet?

Real: every row on this page is a testnet transaction on Reactive Lasna, Base Sepolia or Ethereum Sepolia, linked to its explorer, and the tests run the whole loop. Not yet: mainnet. The deploy script is written and dry-run; it has not been executed. The testnet rows come from a build earlier than HEAD; mainnet deploys HEAD.

What does it cost to keep running?

Not published yet. The long-running instance's measured window is shorter than the 6 hours the meter needs, and a dollar figure needs a second REACT price source. Refused reacts are billed too, which is why the filter runs first and a spam brake bounds it.
