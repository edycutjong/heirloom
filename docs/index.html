Reactive Omni Buildathon · Set It and Forget It

When your wallet goes quiet, silence pays your heir.

If you die with crypto in your own wallet, nobody gets it. Heirloom's reactive contract counts your ordinary transfers of the tokens you list as proof of life. When they stop for the period you set, a grace window opens; any value-carrying transfer of a listed token cancels it. If none comes, two presses anyone can make pay your heir from the vault. No check-in required. No keeper.

How it works

Silence starts the clock.

There is no server. One reactive contract on Lasna listens to your transfers, keeps time on Reactive's Cron and calls back into a small vault on Base.

Silence is the signal

Every value-carrying transfer of an allowlisted token out of your address, on each watched chain, is a visit stamp. The same transfer at $0 is struck through.

Questions a judge would ask.

What counts as being alive?

A value-carrying transfer of a token on your allowlist, out of your address, on a chain you watch; or an action you take on the vault itself. A $0 transfer never counts.

What if I just go travelling for a month?

You choose the silence period at deploy; the long-running testnet instance uses 90 days. Silence first opens a grace window, and any value-carrying transfer of a listed token, or any action of yours on the vault, cancels it. You can withdraw from the vault at any time before release.

Can a stranger fake my life, or cause my release?

Neither a spoof nor an unlisted token counts as life: $0 transfers are refused and logged, and tokens you have not listed are never subscribed. Only your silence can start a release; a sustained spam spray can delay one, never cause one (Remark 1).

What if the Reactive Network stops hearing a chain?

An owner-independent Chainlink ETH/USD feed acts as a canary. If its updates stop arriving, the countdown freezes once, capped, so a short outage cannot trigger a release. The Ethereum feeds measured so far update about hourly (medians 3,612 s and 3,636 s over about 23 h).

What is real, and what is not yet?

Real: every transaction on this page is on a public testnet (Reactive Lasna, Base Sepolia and Ethereum Sepolia, plus one Unichain Sepolia probe), linked to its explorer, and the tests run the whole loop. Not yet: mainnet (scripted, never run; no mainnet transaction exists). The 11-08 demo and the 90-day instance predate the last contract fixes; on 2026-11-22 a fresh instance from 201eb1e, the current contract source, repeated the whole lifecycle.

What does it cost to keep running?

Not published yet. The long-running instance's measured window is shorter than the 6 hours the meter needs, and a dollar figure needs a second REACT price source. Refused reacts are billed too, which is why the filter runs first and a spam brake bounds it.

Your heir needs one link, not your key.

The status page holds the claim: initiateClaim, wait out the claim delay, then finalizeClaim. Anyone may press both steps (in the demo, the owner key did); the payout goes to the heir, or to an address the heir redirects it to.
